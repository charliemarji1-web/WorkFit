About WorkFit

WorkFit is an AI-powered fitness planner that creates personalized workout routines and meal plans based on your goals, schedule, and lifestyle.


Whether you're trying to lose weight, build muscle, gain strength, or improve your overall health, WorkFit helps you stay consistent with a realistic plan made for you.


Our mission is to make fitness easier and more accessible by using artificial intelligence to provide guidance, structure, and motivation for your fitness journey.
