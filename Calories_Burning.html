Calorie-Burning Meals

Grilled Chicken Salad

Recipe:

Season and grill the chicken breast until fully cooked. Slice the chicken and set it aside. Toss mixed greens, cucumber, and tomato in a bowl. Add the chicken and a light vinaigrette, then serve.

Vegetable Stir Fry

Recipe:

Heat a little oil in a pan or wok over high heat. Add chopped vegetables such as broccoli, peppers, and carrots. Stir fry for 5-7 minutes until tender-crisp. Add soy sauce and garlic, then serve on its own or over rice.

Lean Pork with Steamed Vegetables

Recipe:

Season the lean pork with salt, pepper, and herbs. Cook the pork in a pan for about 5-6 minutes per side. Steam a mix of vegetables until tender. Slice the pork and serve alongside the vegetables.

Quinoa Bowl with Roasted Vegetables

Recipe:

Cook quinoa in water for about 15 minutes. Toss chopped vegetables in oil and roast at 400°F for 20-25 minutes. Add the roasted vegetables over the quinoa. Finish with a squeeze of lemon and serve.
