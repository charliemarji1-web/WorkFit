Balanced Meals

Grilled Salmon with Asparagus

Recipe:

Season the salmon with salt, pepper, and lemon. Grill or pan-sear the salmon for 4-5 minutes per side. Toss asparagus in oil and roast or grill until tender. Serve the salmon alongside the asparagus.

Chicken and Vegetable Skewers

Recipe:

Cut chicken and vegetables into bite-sized pieces. Thread them onto skewers, alternating chicken and vegetables. Season with salt, pepper, and herbs. Grill for 10-12 minutes, turning until cooked through.

Beef and Broccoli Stir Fry

Recipe:

Slice the beef thinly and season it. Heat oil in a pan and cook the beef until browned. Add broccoli florets and stir fry for a few minutes. Add soy sauce and garlic, then serve over rice.

Quinoa Salad with Chickpeas

Recipe:

Cook quinoa in water for about 15 minutes, then let it cool. Rinse and drain a can of chickpeas. Combine quinoa, chickpeas, cucumber, and tomato in a bowl. Add olive oil and lemon juice, toss, and serve.
