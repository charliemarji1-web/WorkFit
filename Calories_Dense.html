Calorie Dense Meals


Salmon with Quinoa

Recipe:

Cook quinoa in water for about 15 minutes. Season the salmon with salt, pepper, and your preferred seasoning. Cook salmon in a pan for about 4–5 minutes per side. Serve the salmon over the quinoa.

Chicken Breast With Sweet Potatoes

Recipe:

Cut the sweet potato into cubes. Season the chicken and sweet potatoes. Bake the sweet potatoes at 400°F for about 25–30 minutes. Cook the chicken in a pan for about 6–8 minutes per side. Serve together.

Lean Beef with Brown Rice

Recipe:

Cook brown rice according to the package. Season the lean beef with salt, pepper, and garlic. Cook the beef in a pan until fully cooked. Serve the beef with the brown rice.

Turkey and Avocado Wrap

Recipe:

Place a tortilla on a plate. Add sliced turkey and avocado. Add lettuce, tomato, or other vegetables. Roll the tortilla into a wrap. Cut in half and serve.

Protien Smoothie

Recipe:

Add milk to a blender. Add a banana and your preferred protein powder. Add peanut butter or another ingredient if desired. Blend until smooth. Pour into a cup and serve.
